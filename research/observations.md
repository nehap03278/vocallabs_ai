\# Raw Product Observations — Vocallabs.ai



\## First Impression

\- Product mixes AI voice agents, APIs, and automation tools in one platform.

\- Messaging feels slightly broad across developers and businesses.



\## Observed Issues

\- No immediate interactive voice demo on homepage.

\- Features are listed without clear hierarchy.

\- Outcome value (ROI, savings, conversions) is not clearly visible.



\## Initial Thoughts

\- Product seems powerful but not immediately easy to understand.

\- User needs time to interpret what exactly is being offered.
